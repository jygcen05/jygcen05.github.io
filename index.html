Background

About Me

I am a forestry and conservation professional with comprehensive experience in biodiversity conservation, ecological research, and forest management in Bhutan. Currently serving under the Forest Resources Planning and Management Division, Department of Forests and Park Services, I am engaged in national-level coordination, planning, and technical support related to forest fires, and forest pest and disease management.

As the national focal for forest fire prevention and management, I oversee policy implementation, inter-agency coordination, and capacity-building initiatives to enhance fire resilience across forest landscapes. In addition, I am responsible for monitoring and management of forest pests and diseases, contributing to the health and productivity of Bhutan's forest ecosystems.

Previously, I worked under the Divisional Forest Office, Dagana, where I was actively involved in field-based monitoring, research, and management of forest ecosystems. My professional focus included wildlife monitoring, fish diversity and distribution studies, high conservation value (HCV) area management, community forestry, and human–wildlife interactions. I also contributed to conservation assessments, management effectiveness tracking (METT+), and spatial analysis using GIS and remote sensing tools to support evidence-based decision-making.

Guided by Bhutan's constitutional mandate on environmental stewardship, I remain dedicated to conserving biodiversity, strengthening community livelihoods, and promoting science-based forest and ecosystem management.

Field Studies

Key Research Projects

2025Ichthyology
A Pictorial Guide to the Freshwater Fishes of Dagana
First comprehensive ichthyofauna survey for Dagana Division. Documented 47 freshwater fish species using biodiversity monitoring protocols (BMP) and GIS analysis, establishing the first aquatic biodiversity baseline for the division.
Download PDF
2025Mammalogy
Carnivores Recorded in Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary, Bhutan
Camera-trap and field-based documentation of carnivore assemblages in Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary, providing baseline data for targeted conservation of apex predators and prey species in eastern Bhutan.
Download PDF
2024Entomology
Diversity & Distribution of Ladybird Beetles (Coccinellidae) in Zhemgang
Assessed Coccinellid diversity and habitat distribution across Zhemgang. Published in Bhutan Journal of Natural Resources & Development; contributes to ecosystem health monitoring and IPM strategies.
Download PDF
2023Biodiversity
Rapid Biodiversity Assessment — Dagana
Sampled 29 of 97 NFI plots; recorded 288 floral species (83 families), 40 mammals, 647 birds across 115 species, 144 butterfly species, 15 amphibians, 27 reptiles, and 16 lady beetle species in Dagana Dzongkhag.
Internal Report · DoFPS / IKI Living Landscape
2024Climate
Socio-economic Status & Climate Vulnerability of Dagana
Socio-economic surveys mapped climate vulnerability across Dagana Gewogs. Tsendagang and Drujeygang identified as highly vulnerable; findings informed adaptive capacity-building and community resilience interventions.
Report · DoFPS / IKI Living Landscape
2021Entomology
Six Species of Coccinellidae New to Bhutan (Insecta: Coleoptera)
Published in Oriental Insects. Documented six Coccinellidae species previously unrecorded in Bhutan, expanding the country's known entomological diversity and informing future systematic surveys.
Download PDF
2020Wildlife
Red Panda Survey — Dagana
Field surveys, camera trapping, and habitat assessments documenting red panda presence and habitat preferences in Dagana, feeding into local and national conservation strategies for high conservation value areas.
Survey Report · DoFPS / WWF-Bhutan
2020–2025Monitoring
Biodiversity Monitoring — Insects
Field surveys and insect trapping across multiple habitats in Dagana (2022). Longitudinal documentation of insect communities including key indicator species such as ladybird beetles, supporting ecosystem health assessments.
Reports · DFO, Dagana
